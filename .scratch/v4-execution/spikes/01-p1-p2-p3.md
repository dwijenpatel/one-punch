## Findings

1. **Skill availability self-reports are unreliable.** Under
   `--disable-slash-commands`, 2 of 7 workers said `tdd` was "available" and
   5 said "unavailable". Asking a worker about its own tool surface is not
   evidence. This is moot for the design: worker instructions carry TDD,
   code-style and blast-checklist content inline regardless (plan §8.1 item 4
   / ticket 09).
2. **Workers commit build artifacts when nothing ignores them.** Every worker
   committed `__pycache__/*.pyc` (scratch repo had no `.gitignore`; workers
   used `git add -A`). In real repos `integrate`'s Touches check fails such a
   diff. Preamble rule for ticket 09: stage only files you created or
   changed within `Touches`; never `git add -A`.
3. Parallel speed-up is real at this scale: 4 workers finished in 42 s wall
   against 56 s for one (task-trivial; not a throughput benchmark).
